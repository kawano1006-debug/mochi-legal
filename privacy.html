Privacy Policy

Mochi's Little Days - Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mochi's Little Days content publishing tool (the App) handles information. The App is a personal-use tool built solely to let its developer upload their own original video content to their own TikTok account via the TikTok Content Posting API.

Who uses this App

The App is used only by its developer, for the developer's own TikTok account (mochislittledays). It is not distributed to, or intended for use by, the general public.

Information we access

Through TikTok's official API, the App accesses only the OAuth access token required to publish video content to the developer's own connected TikTok account. The App does not access, collect, or store personal information belonging to other TikTok users or third parties.

Information we store

The App stores an OAuth token locally on the developer's own device, solely to authenticate API requests.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party other than TikTok itself, as required to publish content via the TikTok API.

Data retention and deletion

The developer may revoke the App's access at any time from their TikTok account settings.
